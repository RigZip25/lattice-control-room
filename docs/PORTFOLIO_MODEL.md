# Portfolio model

LATTICE serves one or more organization workspaces whose products have
fundamentally different growth mechanisms. Lafwiron is the proving workspace;
future external customers receive the same isolation guarantees. Shared
infrastructure must not imply a shared funnel or shared data.

## Shared kernel

Every product line uses the same governance primitives:

- versioned product knowledge;
- falsifiable hypotheses and counter-hypotheses;
- immutable observations and provenance;
- bounded experiments;
- explicit capital decisions;
- side-effect authorization;
- outcome measurement and historical lineage;
- brand-scoped budgets, policies and learning.

## Product-specific growth contracts

Each product line supplies a versioned Growth Contract defining its value
event, market-cell grammar, lifecycle stages, constraint metrics, outcome
metrics and eligible playbook families. The contract is configuration plus
domain policy, not a prompt.

### RigZip

- Archetype: local two-sided commercial-asset marketplace.
- Decision unit: geography × commercial asset vertical.
- Critical mechanism: local supply/demand liquidity under operational urgency.
- Early proof: qualified supply, qualified demand and successful matching — not
  registrations alone.

### Evorios

- Archetype: international neighborhood recommerce/rental marketplace.
- Decision unit: neighborhood/metro × entry mode × category cluster.
- Critical mechanism: trust, local density and low-friction first value.
- Entry modes may differ: sell-first, gift/sell-first, rent-first or mixed.

### Books

- Archetype: intellectual-property/content portfolio.
- Decision unit: audience × language × promise/theme × channel.
- Critical mechanism: attention-to-purchase and reader retention/referral.
- There is no two-sided liquidity requirement; marketplace metrics must not be
  inherited.

### Travel platform

- Archetype: travel discovery/planning or transaction platform (exact product
  contract still to be defined).
- Decision unit may combine origin market, destination, traveler intent and
  trip archetype.
- Critical mechanism depends on whether value is itinerary utility, lead
  generation, booking or marketplace transaction.

### Smart navigator

- Archetype: recurring utility product.
- Decision unit may combine geography, mobility context and route/job type.
- Critical mechanism: first-route value, repeated use, reliability and habit.
- Retention and task completion should dominate media engagement metrics.

## Isolation rule

Knowledge transfer is explicit. Universal priors may be shared; category and
product evidence remain scoped. A successful book campaign cannot update
RigZip liquidity policy, and Evorios neighborhood trust evidence cannot become
a universal demographic rule.

Workspace isolation precedes brand isolation. External customer evidence,
budgets, credentials, provider accounts and derived learning are private by
default. A future benchmark network may share only explicitly anonymized and
governed aggregates.

## Architectural consequence

LATTICE is a portfolio decision operating system with product-specific growth
engines. It is not a universal campaign builder and not five separate products
sharing only a dashboard.
