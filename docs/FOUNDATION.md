# Product foundation

## Mission

LATTICE is a market-distribution operating system, initially proven on the
Lafwiron portfolio and architected to become a governed marketing factory for
external product organizations. Its scarce resource is not content volume; it
is governed attention and capital. The system exists to make the next bounded
decision better, faster and more explainable.

## Canonical loop

1. Read a versioned product source and produce a reviewable Brand Package.
2. Identify a product-specific MarketCell and a falsifiable hypothesis.
3. Run the smallest reversible Scout experiment capable of reducing material
   uncertainty.
4. Preserve observations separately from inference and forecast.
5. Decide whether the next exact capital tranche should be approved, modified,
   deferred or rejected.
6. Produce content/distribution work only within the approved scope.
7. Require a separate authorization before any external side effect.
8. Measure downstream value and update future decisions without rewriting the
   historical rationale.

## Product boundaries

- Internal-first product with an explicit workspace boundary so external
  organizations can be supported without redesigning the domain.
- Multi-brand by design; state, budgets, evidence and authority are isolated.
- Provider-neutral core; vendors supply replaceable capabilities.
- PostgreSQL-compatible durability; hosting products do not own correctness.
- No synthetic engagement, fabricated evidence or demographic determinism.
- No live spend, publishing, credential use or bank action without an explicit
  scoped authorization.
- No workspace may read, spend, learn from or act through another workspace
  unless a separately governed transfer contract permits it.

## First success criterion

Given one immutable product snapshot and one candidate MarketCell, LATTICE can
produce a reproducible decision packet that explains:

- what it believed;
- what evidence was observed;
- what remains uncertain;
- why an exact next tranche was selected;
- what content work follows;
- why external distribution is still allowed or blocked.

The same inputs and policy version must produce the same decision identity and
result.

## Deferred decisions

- Production hosting and authentication provider.
- SaaS packaging, billing, onboarding and commercial plan design.
- Live advertising, publishing and banking integrations.
- Final statistical model for causal attribution.
- Automatic approval thresholds beyond non-production fixtures.
- Portfolio-wide optimization objective after real downstream data exists.
