# Finance/Treasury and Venture boundary

## Finance & Treasury

Owns settled financial truth and capital safety:

- cash and authorized funding sources;
- immutable double-entry ledger;
- workspace and brand budget envelopes;
- commitments, reservations, spend, refunds and provider costs;
- revenue, contribution, reinvestment policy and runway;
- reconciliation and accounting periods;
- explicit banking/funding authority.

Treasury answers: **what capital exists, what is already encumbered, and what
may legally and operationally be deployed?**

## Venture

Owns the internal market for uncertain growth investments:

- opportunity and hypothesis queue;
- exact requested/recommended tranches;
- expected marginal value and uncertainty;
- alternative uses and opportunity cost;
- exploration reserve and concentration limits;
- approve, modify, defer and reject decisions;
- frozen forecast and post-funding evaluation;
- Scout/model calibration.

Venture answers: **which eligible opportunity deserves the next bounded
tranche and why?**

## Transaction boundary

`CapitalRequest -> VentureDecision -> TreasuryReservation -> AllocationTicket -> BudgetLedger entries -> OutcomeEvaluation`

- A Venture decision cannot create money or call a payment/ad provider.
- Treasury cannot turn an unapproved hypothesis into a marketing strategy.
- A reservation is not spend.
- Spend must reference an allocation ticket and authorized execution.
- Unused reservations are released; settled spend remains consumed.
- Forecasts remain immutable and are compared with actuals after settlement.
