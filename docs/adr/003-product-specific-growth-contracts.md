# ADR 003: Product-specific growth contracts over a shared kernel

Status: accepted

## Decision

Each product line registers a versioned Growth Contract. Shared LATTICE
services govern evidence, experiments, capital, authorization, execution and
audit. The contract defines product-specific value events, MarketCell grammar,
lifecycle, metrics and eligible playbooks.

## Why

RigZip and Evorios are liquidity-dependent marketplaces, books are an IP/content
business, travel may be discovery or transactional, and a navigator is a
recurring utility. Forcing them into one funnel would optimize convenient but
meaningless common metrics.

## Guardrail

Cross-product learning requires an explicit transfer policy identifying the
source scope, target scope, invariant being transferred and evidence grade.
