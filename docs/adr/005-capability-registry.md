# ADR 005: LATTICE owns expertise; providers supply capabilities

Status: accepted

## Decision

Strategy, evidence, policy, capital allocation, quality thresholds and learning
remain inside LATTICE. Media generators, research services, ad networks,
publishing tools, CRM systems and analytics sources integrate through a
versioned Capability Registry and provider conformance contracts.

## Consequences

- New channels and generation models can be added without changing the core
  decision loop.
- Channel-specific metrics remain native and map to product value events.
- Provider claims do not become canonical evidence without validation.
- A provider can be degraded, replaced or disabled without losing strategy and
  historical lineage.
